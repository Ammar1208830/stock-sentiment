# Third-party components

The vendored UI source in `src/components/ui/` comes from [shadcn/ui](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui), installed with its official CLI using the `new-york` registry style. Its license is reproduced in `licenses/shadcn-ui-MIT.txt`.

Icons are provided by the installed [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide) `lucide-react` package under its ISC license and the included Feather icon notice. Its license is reproduced in `licenses/lucide-LICENSE.txt`.

React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Radix primitives and the remaining packages retain their respective licenses in `node_modules`. No third-party logo is used as the project's identity; `public/logo.svg` is a custom project asset.
